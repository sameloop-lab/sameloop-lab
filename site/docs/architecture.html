Architecture

The functional contract is shared; native middleware mechanisms remain visible. AFB subcalls, KUKSA signal operations, OpenHarmony IPC and webOS LS2 are not a single interchangeable protocol. Read known deviations before comparing.

The current implementations have different packaging and simulation boundaries. The plant is used by KUKSA today, not yet by all stacks. The diagram gallery distinguishes delivered lab profiles from proposals. Red project names identify proposed components; dashed links indicate future work in architecture diagrams. Solid lines in functional diagrams express relationships, not executed tests. French diagram text is retained from the development dossier.

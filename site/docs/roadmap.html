Roadmap

  1. Reproduce AFB/KUKSA component tests on the maintainer's workstation and register artifacts.
  2. Connect one graphical UI to AFB, then KUKSA; test settings, subscriptions and disconnects.
  3. Decide on the common numerical model and align comparison fixtures.
  4. Add common fault scenarios; qualify Toxiproxy and a focused MockServer experiment.
  5. Qualify one Zephyr board/link, with an explicit controller owner.
  6. Prototype OpenHarmony and webOS on pinned SDK/images before claiming support.
  7. Qualify permissions, TLS modes and packaging separately.

Only the first-stage component evidence exists today. CommonAPI and Qt remain optional. Individual security, hardware, testing and beginner-onboarding workstreams may grow independently; no colleague commitments or speaker assignments are implied.
